> This CPS describes how the SE Access Certificate Authority issues and manages
> Wallet-Relying Party Access Certificates (WRPAC) in accordance with
> Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), Commission Implementing Regulation
> (EU) 2025/848, ETSI TS 119 411-8, and ETSI TS 119 475.

---

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Overview
The SE Access Certificate Authority ("the CA") is operated by «SUNET» acting as a
Trust Service Provider ("the TSP") designated by «the Swedish supervisory body» to
issue access certificates to wallet-relying parties (WRPs) registered in the Swedish
national register of wallet-relying parties.

### 1.2 Document name and identification
This CPS is identified by OID «0.4.0.194118.x.y». Certificates issued under it
assert one of the Certificate Policies defined in ETSI TS 119 411-8 clause 5.3:

| Policy | OID | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| NCP-n-eudiwrp | 0.4.0.194118.1.1 | WRPAC for natural persons (e-signature) |
| NCP-l-eudiwrp | 0.4.0.194118.1.2 | WRPAC for legal persons (e-seal) |
| QCP-n-eudiwrp | 0.4.0.194118.1.3 | Qualified WRPAC for natural persons |
| QCP-l-eudiwrp | 0.4.0.194118.1.4 | Qualified WRPAC for legal persons |

The CA does **not** assert qualified status unless explicitly stated for the
QCP-* policies.

### 1.3 PKI participants
- **Certification Authority:** SE Access Certificate Authority.
- **Registration Authority:** the registrar of wallet-relying parties maintaining
  the Swedish national register; identity proofing follows ETSI TS 119 461.
- **Subjects:** wallet-relying parties (legal or natural persons) with a valid
  entry in the national register.
- **Relying parties:** EUDI Wallet instances and other parties validating WRPACs.

### 1.4 Certificate usage
WRPACs are used by a registered WRP (or an intermediary acting on its behalf) to
authenticate to an EUDI Wallet and to apply advanced electronic signatures/seals
to wallet requests. Any other use is prohibited.

### 1.5 Policy administration
Maintained by «SUNET PKI Policy Authority», «pki@sunet.se». Changes are approved
by the Policy Authority and published at the location above.

## 3. Identification and authentication

### 3.1 Naming
Subject DNs follow ETSI EN 319 412-2 (natural persons) / EN 319 412-3 (legal
persons) and the mapping in ETSI TS 119 475 clause 5.1. Each WRPAC includes
`organizationIdentifier` per EN 319 412-1 §5.1.4.

### 3.2 Initial identity validation
Identity of the WRP and its legal representatives is verified by the registrar to
the level required by ETSI TS 119 461 before registration. The CA issues only on
the basis of a verified register entry; it does not re-perform identity proofing
where the registrar has confirmed it (TS 119 411-8 clause 4.6.3).

### 3.3 Re-key / revocation requests
Authenticated against the registered WRP contact data; see §4.

---

## 4. Certificate life-cycle operational requirements

### 4.1 Application & issuance
A WRP submits a CSR. The CA validates that the CSR subject matches the register
entry, builds the authoritative extensions (Certificate Policies + cpsURI, SAN
contact data, key usage via the eudiwrp profile), and signs.

### 4.2 Validity
WRPACs are issued with a maximum validity of «12 months».

### 4.3 Revocation and status
Revocation is available on request from the WRP, the registrar, or the supervisory
body, and is reflected via CRL and OCSP «within 24 hours / 60 minutes». Reasons
include key compromise, loss of registration, or inaccurate data.

---

## 5. Facility, management and operational controls
Physical, procedural, and personnel controls meet ETSI EN 319 401 and
EN 319 411-1 clause 6.4. CA private keys are held in «a FIPS 140-2 Level 3 / EN
419221-5 certified HSM». Audit logs are retained for «7 years».

---

## 6. Technical security controls
- CA key pair: «ECDSA P-384», generated and stored in the HSM.
- Subject keys: generated by the WRP; the CA does not generate or escrow subject
  private keys.
- Signing algorithm: «ecdsa-with-SHA512».

---

## 7. Certificate, CRL and OCSP profiles
- **Certificate profile:** ETSI TS 119 411-8 clause 6.6.1 and ETSI TS 119 475
  (WRPAC columns of Tables 1 and 3). Each WRPAC contains exactly one of the
  policy OIDs in §1.2 together with this cpsURI; SAN carries the WRP's supportURI,
  and optionally phone and email; BasicConstraints CA:FALSE; KeyUsage
  digitalSignature. **Entitlement information is conveyed in the registration
  certificate (WRPRC), not in the WRPAC.**
- **CRL profile:** EN 319 411-1 clause 6.6.2.
- **OCSP profile:** EN 319 411-1 clause 6.6.3.
